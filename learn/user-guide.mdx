Everything starts with a vault's supported input token. Deposit for vTokens, then choose discounted ST to sell future points exposure or EPT to buy concentrated points exposure.

---

## Deposit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a vault">
    Review the input token, supported network, underlying protocol, yield behavior, points program, fees, and withdrawal terms. Vaults can generate yield, points, or both.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deposit the supported input token">
    Enter your amount and approve the token if required. The app shows the current NAV and expected vTokens before confirmation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive vToken and choose a series">
    vToken is the entry and settlement asset for ST/EPT markets. Select the maturity that matches the period over which you want to sell or buy points exposure.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Trade ST or EPT

Select a **series** with the maturity date you want. The same vToken can have multiple series, each with its own ST and EPT.

The ST/EPT book is quoted in APR. Read the [Orderbook Guide](/learn/orderbook-guide) before trading if you want the full pricing and order-flow model.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Points Side (EPT)" icon="chart-line">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select a series">
        Review the series maturity, EPT fee, time remaining, and points-program assumptions.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open the EPT orderbook">
        Navigate to the EPT/vToken view for that series.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Place an order">
        Pay with vTokens to buy EPT. Use a market order for immediate execution within your slippage limit, or a limit order for a selected APR.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Hold and accrue credits">
        Credits accrue automatically while you hold EPT. Your counter starts when you receive the EPT; the seller keeps credits accumulated earlier.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Claim CreditTokens">
        After the market operator submits point weights on-chain, the contracts automatically issue CreditTokens based on your credit share and the applicable EPT fee.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Underlying Side (ST)" icon="vault">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select a series">
        Review the maturity, ST/vToken discount, vault NAV behavior, and any underlying yield.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open the ST orderbook">
        Navigate to the ST/vToken view for that series.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Place an order">
        Pay with vTokens to buy ST below 1 vToken. Economically, you are selling the future points exposure and keeping the underlying side without points exposure. Use a market order for immediate execution within your slippage limit, or a limit order for a selected APR.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Hold through maturity">
        ST carries the underlying side, including any yield in the vault position but without the points exposure. For a zero-yield vault, it is simply the underlying-token side.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Redeem">
        After maturity, redeem each ST for one vToken.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

---

## Exit Before Maturity

You do not have to hold ST or EPT until maturity:

- **Sell EPT** for vToken. You keep credits accumulated before the sale; the buyer starts accruing from the transfer.
- **Sell ST** for vToken at the available market price.
- **Recombine** equal amounts of ST and EPT into vToken where the app provides that action.

Execution depends on available liquidity and can be subject to price impact, slippage, and fees.

---

## Withdraw vToken

Withdrawal returns the vault's input token.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review">
    Check the network, input token, recipient, estimated amount, fees, and processing time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request">
    Submit the vToken amount. When the vTokens are burned, they stop carrying yield and accruing credits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete">
    Follow any finalization step shown by the app and receive the vault's input token when processing finishes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Withdrawal timing and availability are vault-specific. If you hold ST or EPT, first sell or recombine them into vToken.

---

## At and After Maturity

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="vToken Holders" icon="wallet">
    vToken is not tied to a single series maturity. It remains the base vault share, follows the vault's NAV, and can continue accruing credits according to active periods.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="ST Holders" icon="vault">
    Redeem ST 1:1 for vToken after maturity. Redemption burns the ST.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="EPT Holders" icon="star">
    EPT stops accruing new credits for that series at maturity. Claim available CreditTokens according to finalized periods and the fee shown for the series.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

---

## Use CreditTokens

CreditTokens are issued automatically from accrued credit shares after the market operator submits point weights on-chain. They are vault-specific, used for reward distribution, and persist across series from the same vault.

Open **Swaps** to buy or sell CreditTokens using direct prices, market orders, or limit orders.

Read [Trading CreditTokens](/learn/credit-token-trading) for the full flow.

---

## Quick Reference

| Action | When available |
|---|---|
| Deposit the vault input token for vToken | According to vault availability |
| Buy or sell ST/EPT | Before the selected series matures |
| Accrue credits on EPT | While held and before series maturity |
| Redeem ST for vToken | At or after series maturity |
| Claim CreditTokens | As credit periods become claimable |
| Withdraw vToken | According to vault-specific rules |
| Trade CreditTokens | Subject to market availability |
