## What Is a PointsToken

Each supported perp DEX has its own PointsToken, representing that exchange's loyalty points 1:1.

Additional PointsTokens are created as new exchanges are integrated. One PointsToken exists per exchange, shared across all vaults and series on that exchange.

---

## How Claiming Works

As points are distributed on the exchange each week, holders with accumulated credits can claim their share as PointsTokens. Both vToken and EPT holders earn credits and can claim.

1. Weekly points are reported for the vault
2. You claim based on your credit share

---

## PointsTokens Persist Across Series

Unlike ST and EPT, which are created fresh for each series, PointsTokens accumulate. Your PT from one series and your PT from another are the **same token**. They stack in your wallet.

PointsTokens are standard ERC20 tokens. You can hold, transfer, or trade them freely.

---

## Post-TGE: Burn for Airdrop Tokens

When an exchange conducts its <Tooltip tip="Token Generation Event. When a perp DEX launches its token and distributes airdrops to points holders.">TGE</Tooltip>:

1. The exchange distributes airdrop tokens
2. A conversion rate is set based on total airdrop tokens and total PointsToken supply
3. You burn your PointsTokens to receive airdrop tokens at that rate

**Example:** Assume the points token is called PT. You hold 1,000 PT. The exchange airdrops 50,000 tokens against a total PT supply of 100,000.

$$\text{conversion rate} = \frac{50{,}000 \text{ airdrop tokens}}{100{,}000 \text{ PT}} = 0.5$$

You burn 1,000 PT and receive 500 airdrop tokens.
