A **series** is a fixed-maturity market built on top of a vToken.

Each vToken can have multiple series over time. Every series has:

- a fixed **maturity date**
- its own **ST**
- its own **EPT**

ST and EPT only apply to that specific series. When the series reaches maturity, that series stops accruing new time-based exposure.

## Before Maturity

Before maturity, users can:

- buy **ST** for the underlying-token side, including any yield but without points exposure, until maturity
- buy **EPT** for points exposure until maturity

## At Maturity

At maturity:

- **ST** redeems back into vToken
- **EPT** stops accruing new credits for that series and can be used to claim CreditTokens based on accrued credits
- **vToken** continues as the base vault share
