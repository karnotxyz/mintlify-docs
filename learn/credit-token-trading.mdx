CreditTokens trade against **USDC** on the orderbook in **Swaps**. Choose a CreditToken, enter the amount, and buy or sell.

## Order Types

- **Market order:** buy or sell immediately at the available orderbook price.
- **Limit order:** choose a USDC price and amount. The order remains open until it is filled or cancelled.

Fees are shown before you confirm the trade.
