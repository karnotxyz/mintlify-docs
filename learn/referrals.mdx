ArcX referrals reward both sides of a valid invite — **you** (the referrer) and **your friend** (the referee).

The current program gives **10% extra rewards**: you earn a share of your friend's ArcX vault activity, and your friend earns extra rewards too.

Rewards are tracked in kind:

- **Yield (USDC)** rewards are paid in USDC.
- **Points (CreditToken)** rewards are paid in the CreditToken for the relevant vault.

---

## Who Can Refer

To create a referral code, a wallet must have at least **$50 deposited in ArcX vaults**.

After meeting the $50 deposit requirement, you can create **one** referral code from the [Referrals page](https://app.arcx.trade/referrals).

```text
ARCX1
```

---

## How Friends Qualify

Your friend must accept the referral **before their first ArcX vault deposit** — by opening your referral link, or entering your code on the [Referrals page](https://app.arcx.trade/referrals). Accepting takes a quick wallet signature; it is not a token approval and never moves funds.

<Warning>
  If a wallet has already deposited into an ArcX vault, it cannot accept a new referral.
</Warning>

---

## How Rewards Work

Referral rewards accrue from ArcX vault activity after the referral is accepted.

Yield rewards and Points rewards are handled separately because they are different assets. A single referred wallet can deposit into multiple vaults, and each vault can produce its own Yield (USDC) and Points (CreditToken) reward stream.

Referral statistics and rewards can update with a delay while ArcX indexes on-chain activity and processes reward jobs.

---

## Claiming Rewards

Claimable rewards appear on the [Referrals page](https://app.arcx.trade/referrals) once funded. Claims are token-specific, so Yield (USDC) and each vault's Points (CreditToken) rewards are claimed separately.

---

## Rules

- A referral must be accepted before the invited wallet's first ArcX vault deposit.
- A wallet can have only one inviter.
- A wallet can create only one referral code.
- The inviter must have at least **$50 deposited in ArcX vaults** before their code can be created and used.
- Self-referrals do not qualify.
- Rewards apply only to ArcX vault activity.
- ArcX reserves the right to modify rates, rules, eligibility, supported markets, or cancel the program at any time.
