## Rewards Are Not Promised

CreditTokens are issued automatically from finalised ArcX credit shares after the market operator submits point weights on-chain. They can be used by a vault's configured reward-distribution logic.

No TGE, airdrop, reward amount, conversion ratio, eligibility, timing, transferability, or market value is promised. CreditTokens are not points recorded by the underlying protocol and do not create a guaranteed direct claim against that protocol.

## CreditToken Trading

CreditToken prices are set by market participants. Market orders can fail, and limit orders can remain open or partially filled while funds stay escrowed.

An interface metric such as “cost per point” is shorthand for comparing market exposure. It does not mean one CreditToken equals one underlying-protocol point or guarantee a future reward value.
