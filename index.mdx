Deposit USDC into a vault, receive a **vToken** — a vault share that earns fixed yield and exchange points. Hold it for the combined return, or buy **ST** (yield) or **EPT** (points) on the orderbook to concentrate into the side you want.

Points farmers buy EPT for leveraged points exposure. Yield seekers buy discounted ST for fixed returns. Or just hold vToken and earn both.
