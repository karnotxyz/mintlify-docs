---
title: "ArcX Documentation"
description: "ArcX strips the points exposure from vault positions, allowing the underlying and points sides to trade independently."
icon: "book-open"
---

Deposit a vault's supported input token and receive a **vToken**, then separate the position into **ST** and **EPT**. Move into discounted ST to sell future points exposure while keeping the underlying side, including any yield but without the points exposure. Buy EPT to get concentrated points exposure without funding the underlying side.

EPT accrues credits. After the market operator submits point weights on-chain, the contracts automatically issue **CreditTokens** according to each holder's credit share.

CreditTokens are transferable and can be traded independently.
