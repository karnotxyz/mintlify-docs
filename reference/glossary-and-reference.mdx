## Tokens

| Token | What it is | Tradeable | Redeemable for | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **vToken** | Base vault share | Yes, like a normal ERC20 | The vault's input token | Per vault |
| **ST** | Underlying side of a series, including any yield but without points exposure | Yes (orderbook) | Redeems back into vToken at maturity | Per vault, per series |
| **EPT** | Points side of a series | Yes (orderbook) | CreditTokens based on accrued credits | Per vault, per series |
| **CreditToken** | Vault-specific token issued automatically after the market operator submits point weights on-chain | Yes (direct-price market) | Reward sharing for that vault | Per vault, persists across series |

### Split Equation

$$1 \text{ vToken} = 1 \text{ ST} + 1 \text{ EPT}$$

The matching engine enforces this parity internally when orders match.

## Key Equations

| Equation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| shares = deposit / NAV | vToken shares minted on deposit |
| NAV = vault value / total shares | Per-share value of the vault |
| 1 vToken = 1 ST + 1 EPT | Structural relationship (engine handles internally) |
| your CreditTokens = Σ (your credits_w / total credits_w) × period_weight_w | CreditToken issuance by credit share and point weight |

### GCI (Global Credit Index)

Credits per share since series inception, computed on-chain from NAV:

$$\text{GCI increment} = \frac{\text{NAV}_{start} + \text{NAV}_{end}}{2} \times \text{days}$$

$$\text{your credits} = \text{your shares} \times \text{GCI increment}$$

If NAV is constant at 1, this reduces to:

$$\text{your credits} = \text{your shares} \times \text{days}$$

Both vToken and EPT holders earn credits at the same rate. After the market operator submits point weights on-chain, the contracts automatically issue CreditTokens from the period's credit shares.

## Trading Models

| Market | Quote | Order behavior |
|---|---|---|
| **ST/EPT** | APR | Series-specific trading against vToken |
| **CreditToken** | Quote token per CreditToken | Market orders are fill-or-kill; limit orders can fill partially |

## Fees

- **vToken performance fee** — a cut of the yield and CreditTokens distributed through the vToken
- **EPT fee** — a fee on CreditTokens received by EPT holders for the points-side exposure ArcX provides
- **CreditToken maker/taker fee** — a fee applied to CreditToken orderbook fills

Exact values vary by market and can be seen in the app.

---

## Series

**vToken** behaves like a vault share. Deposits and withdrawals follow vault-specific terms.

**ST** and **EPT** are created as part of fixed-maturity series.

Each vToken can have multiple series over time. Every series has:

- its own maturity date
- its own **ST**
- its own **EPT**

At maturity:

- **ST** redeems back into vToken
- **EPT** stops accruing credits for that series and can be used to claim CreditTokens
- **vToken** continues as the base vault share

## Withdrawal

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Review** | Confirm the vault's input token, network, recipient, fees, estimated amount, and processing time. |
| **Request** | Submit vToken for withdrawal. Once burned, it stops carrying yield and accruing credits. |
| **Complete** | Follow any finalization step shown in the app and receive the vault's input token when processing finishes. |

Withdrawal availability and timing are vault-specific.
